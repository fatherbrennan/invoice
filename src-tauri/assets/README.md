# BPAY logo

`bpay-mono.png` is the monochrome portrait BPAY logo, extracted without changing
its proportions from Figure 1.2, page 5 of the BPAY Brand Identity Guidelines
published by ANZ:
https://www.anz.com/BPAY/pdf/BPAY_Brand_Guidelines_new.pdf

It is rendered about 16 mm high with 2 mm clear space in monochrome invoices.
BPAY is a registered trademark of BPAY Pty Ltd. Use is for registered BPAY billers;
their financial institution must approve the final bill configuration.
Current guidance: https://brandportalbpay.auspayplus.com.au/
